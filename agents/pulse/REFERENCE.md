# Pulse — Reference & Tools

## Analytics Toolkit
- **Repliz API:** schedule performance data
- **Instagram/Threads metrics:** engagement rate, reach, impressions
- **LinkedIn metrics:** impressions, clicks, engagement
- **Comparison tools:** period-over-period, format-vs-format

## Metrics to Track
| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Engagement Rate | (likes+comments+shares)/reach | >3% IG, >1% LinkedIn |
| Reach | Unique accounts seen | Growth trend |
| Saves | IG saves (high-intent signal) | Growing |
| CTA Clicks | Link clicks to whitepaper.site | Growing |
| Follower Growth | Net new followers/week | Positive trend |
