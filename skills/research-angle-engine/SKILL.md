# Research Angle Engine

Use this skill before writing content.

## Goal
Turn raw project input into a sharp research brief that helps content, design, publishing, and sales follow-up.

## Outputs
Return these sections:
1. Business context
2. Likely buyer
3. Audience pain points
4. Message angle options
5. Strongest hook direction
6. Trust proof available in the assets
7. CTA direction
8. Risks / weak angles to avoid

## Rules
- Prefer concrete B2B usefulness over generic inspiration.
- Do not force a weak angle if the assets do not support it.
- Highlight what helps inquiry intent and what does not.
- Keep the brief useful for DONI, Sora, Relay, and Exel.
