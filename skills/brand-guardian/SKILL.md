# Brand Guardian

Use this skill whenever content or design should be reviewed against brand standards.

## Goal
Protect consistency so Whitepaper outputs look and sound like Whitepaper, not generic templates.

## Review checklist
Check:
- tone: direct, confident, professional, warm, transparent
- clarity: short, clean, not wordy
- visual feel: minimal, premium, whitespace-heavy
- palette fit: white, off-white, charcoal, warm neutral, restrained gold accent
- discipline: no noisy layout, no fake hype, no weak filler visuals
- commercial fit: supports trust, inquiry intent, and premium positioning

## Outputs
Return:
1. What fits the brand
2. What breaks the brand
3. Fixes needed
4. Final go / revise recommendation

## Rules
- Reject low-meaning visuals as hero assets.
- Reject copy that sounds vague, generic, or over-sold.
- Prefer premium restraint over decorative excess.
