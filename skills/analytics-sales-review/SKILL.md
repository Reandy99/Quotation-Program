# Analytics Sales Review

Use this skill after posts are live or when reviewing a content batch.

## Goal
Judge whether content is commercially useful, not merely visible.

## Review areas
Check:
- trust signal strength
- audience relevance
- inquiry intent strength
- CTA effectiveness
- whether design and copy support premium positioning
- whether the post likely helps leads or proposal conversations

## Outputs
Return:
1. What worked
2. What weakened performance
3. Trust impact
4. Inquiry / sales impact
5. Recommended next iteration
6. Continue / revise / stop verdict

## Rules
- Do not stop at likes and reach.
- Distinguish visibility from commercial usefulness.
- Say clearly when a post looks good but is unlikely to generate business value.
