# Publishing QC System

Use this skill before scheduling or approving a post.

## Goal
Prevent weak, broken, or misaligned posts from reaching the platform.

## Review points
Check:
- correct platform fit
- caption readability
- line breaks / formatting hygiene
- media order and hero-image strength
- brand fit
- CTA fit
- schedule readiness
- likely publish risks

## Outputs
Return:
1. Platform
2. Hero asset judgment
3. Caption QA
4. Formatting QA
5. Risk flags
6. Approve / revise

## Rules
- Do not approve low-meaning images as the lead asset.
- Do not approve weak hooks just because the schedule is full.
- Flag caption-media mismatch immediately.
- Prefer delaying a weak post over publishing it anyway.
