## Target Audience
**Target companies:** B2B companies and organizations running seminars, conferences, town halls, internal events, and business forums (typically mid-market to enterprise).

**Decision-makers:** Marketing/Comms, PR, HR/Employer Branding, Internal Communications, Event Committee/EO, sometimes CEO/GM.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Reduce follow-up friction by having proof-ready visuals quickly after the event.

**Use cases:**
- Event recap (LinkedIn/IG/Threads)

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Companies invest heavily in events but end up with documentation that is late, unusable, or too generic to drive trust and action.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- No clear shot list or objective, just “capture everything”.
- Delivery is slow or unorganized, teams cannot find what they need.
- Photos look fine technically but lack business meaning (no context, no people, no branding).

**What it costs them:** Missed PR window, weak employer branding, lower trust, slower sales follow-up, and wasted event spend.

**Emotional tension:** Fear of looking unprofessional, stress near deadlines, uncertainty about vendor reliability.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Freelance event photographers/videographers — often inconsistent standards, unclear delivery, and variable turnaround.

**Secondary:** Event agencies providing bundled documentation — may be less specialized in premium visual output.

**Indirect:** In-house team or “phone footage” — fast but inconsistent quality and low trust signal.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Clear delivery standards and organized outputs
- Fast turnaround (set expectation up front)
- Visuals optimized for business use (PR, internal, employer brand, sales)
- Premium, clean, minimal look and tone

**How we do it differently:** We treat documentation as a content system, not an archive.

**Why that's better:** Teams can use the assets immediately and repeatedly, which increases trust and reduces follow-up friction.

**Why customers choose us:** Reliability + premium restraint + speed + business usefulness.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Late delivery, messy folders, missed key moments, low-trust visuals.

**Pull:** Clear standards, premium outputs, fast turnaround, content-library mindset.

**Habit:** Reusing the same vendor out of convenience.

**Anxiety:** Fear of trying a new vendor and risking an important event.
